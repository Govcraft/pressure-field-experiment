\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

This work sits at the end of a single lineage running from digital pheromones to language-model agents, through five stages: the digital-pheromone abstraction of classic stigmergy; its reframing as a continuous field between agents and equations; the environment as a first-class coordination substrate; the symmetric extension that lets the environment act back on agents; and recent environment-mediated coordination for foundation-model multi-agent systems. The symmetric-stigmergic schema~\cite{parunak2025insects} already unifies these stages, so we position the contribution against each in turn.

\subsection{Digital Pheromones and Stigmergic Optimization}

Grass\'{e} named stigmergy to explain termite construction, where deposited material attracts further deposits and complex structure emerges with no agent holding a global plan~\cite{grasse1959stigmergie}. Parunak abstracted this into engineering principles for artificial multi-agent systems, identifying deposition, aggregation, and evaporation as the operators that make pheromone coordination robust and scalable~\cite{parunak1997ant}. Dorigo and colleagues turned the same insight into ant colony optimization, where artificial pheromone trails guide combinatorial search through positive feedback on good paths and evaporation of stale ones~\cite{dorigo1996ant,dorigo1997acs,dorigo2004acobook}. The approach extends to telecommunications routing~\cite{bonabeau1998iata} and rests on the broader account of self-organization in biological systems~\cite{camazine2001selforg}. We inherit the deposition-and-evaporation core directly: the example bank and the rejected-patch store are pheromone fields with per-tick evaporation, and the sensed pressure field is the aggregate signal agents follow. Two task-driven differences from classic ant systems remain: the action space is open-ended natural-language patches rather than enumerated moves, and the agents reason rather than react.

\subsection{Pheromone Fields Between Agents and Equations}

Treating per-location pheromone variables as a continuous field places stigmergic models on a continuum between agent-based and equation-based modeling. Parunak showed analytically that pheromone coordination is intermediate between agent-based and mean-field models, with a model's position set by its pheromone parameters, notably the propagation factor~\cite{parunak2011mean}. Field-based middleware made the abstraction programmable: the TOTA model distributes computational fields over a network that agents sense and follow~\cite{mamei2009tota}, and the field metaphor recurs across catalogues of biology-inspired design patterns for distributed computing~\cite{babaoglu2006patterns}. Our pressure field is such a field, and its one-hop propagation operator instantiates that continuum control~\cite{parunak2011mean}: raising $\kappa$ spreads activation further along the temporal chain. We keep propagation deliberately shallow, so the field stays near the agent-based end where individual patches remain the unit of action.

\subsection{The Environment as a First-Class Substrate}

A separate strand argued that the environment, not the agents alone, carries coordination and should be engineered as a first-class entity. The E4MAS research roadmap formalized the environment as an explicit design dimension mediating agent access to resources and to one another~\cite{weyns2007environment}. Methodologies for engineering self-organizing systems named gradient fields as a reusable coordination pattern and catalogued the mechanisms through which global order emerges from local rules: positive and negative feedback, randomness, and repeated local interaction~\cite{dewolf2005engineering,serugendo2005selforganisation}. The pattern has seen industrial use: stigmergic coordination drives manufacturing execution control~\cite{hadeli2004stigmergy,valckenaers2015design}, digital pheromones coordinate swarming unmanned vehicles~\cite{sauter2005pheromones}, and delegate multi-agent systems route traffic anticipatorily over a pheromone substrate~\cite{claes2011anticipatory}. Our agents hold no model of one another and coordinate solely by reading and writing the shared artifact, so the environment is the entire coordination channel. We extend this strand with a foundation-model realization in which the environment also reallocates agent capability, made precise in the next stage.

\subsection{Symmetric Stigmergy and the BDI Bridge}

The symmetric extension~\cite{parunak2025insects} supplies the arrow classic stigmergy lacks, letting the environment update agent state, and adds agent-side analogues of the environment's update, dynamics, and propagation operators. A single schema then spans a continuum from ant-like agents through symmetric stigmergy to belief-desire-intention agents~\cite{bratman1987intentions,raogeorgeff1995bdi}, building on polyagent and hierarchical-task-network stigmergy that deploys reasoning-capable agents against a stigmergic substrate~\cite{brueckner2000return,brueckner2009saso}. We place foundation-model actors at the symmetric-stigmergy point of that continuum and give the primitive-by-primitive mapping in Section~\ref{sec:symmetric-stigmergy}. Joint-intentions accounts of teamwork require each agent to hold and reason about mutual beliefs over the others' commitments~\cite{cohen1991teamwork}; symmetric-stigmergic coordination requires none, since the shared field carries everything the agents need to align.

\subsection{Environment-Mediated Coordination for Foundation-Model Agents}

Most foundation-model multi-agent systems coordinate through explicit dialogue: agents pass messages, assume roles, and decompose tasks hierarchically. AutoGen formalizes conversation among configurable agents~\cite{wu2023autogen}; MetaGPT encodes standard operating procedures into role workflows over a shared message pool~\cite{hong2024metagpt}; CAMEL scripts role-play between paired agents~\cite{li2023camel}; ChatDev structures a software team as role-based chat chains over a shared codebase~\cite{qian2024chatdev}; and multi-agent debate converges instances through rounds of explicit critique~\cite{du2024debate}. These designs scale poorly. Coordination overhead can turn multi-agent setups into a net cost on sequential tasks~\cite{kim2025scaling}, most inter-agent messages are prunable without loss~\cite{zhang2025agentprune}, and performance gains follow a saturating curve in agent count~\cite{qian2025macnet}. Surveys map the same space and note that the environment-mediated, distributed-and-cooperative quadrant remains thinly populated~\cite{guo2024llmmas_survey}.

A smaller body of work coordinates foundation-model agents through the environment rather than through messages. Generative agents organize joint activity through shared environmental co-presence and a retrievable memory stream rather than broadcast~\cite{park2023generative}; coordination benchmarks rank environment-grounded coordination above theory-of-mind reasoning between agents~\cite{agashe2025coordination}; blackboard architectures let agents coordinate solely through a public store~\cite{han2025blackboard}; agents develop implicit numerical signaling that functions like pheromone traces even without language~\cite{buscemi2025implicit}; and information-theoretic accounts measure emergence under group-level feedback with no direct communication~\cite{riedl2025emergent}. Stigmergy proper is reaching this setting too: language-model agents have been run as pheromone-following foragers~\cite{jimenez2025swarm}, digital pheromones bridge independent reinforcement learners~\cite{xu2022stigmergic}, and decayed pheromone maps coordinate robot swarms where conventional multi-agent reinforcement learning fails~\cite{aina2025smadrl}. Norm-emergence work offers a complementary route, aligning behavior through shared normative expectations rather than fields~\cite{ren2024norms,savarimuthu2024norms}, and organization-generation work automates the role structures we instead remove~\cite{amaral2023organisations}. Within this strand, prior systems coordinate through the environment but leave its influence on agents implicit; we make that influence an explicit operator and map a complete artifact-refinement loop onto the symmetric-stigmergic primitives. Prior work suggests but does not establish that small reasoning models struggle with purely emergent decentralized coordination~\cite{valmeekam2023planning}; Section~\ref{sec:experiments} locates the regime in which environment-mediated coordination nonetheless outperforms hierarchical control.
